Historical kana sound-change rules (内閣告示「現代かなづかい」/「現代仮名遣い」)
==============================================================================

Component : app/src/main/assets/variants/kana_sound_changes.txt
            (paired with variants/kana_sound_changes.PROVENANCE.txt)
Generator : tools/build_kana_sound_changes.py

What it is
----------
The 旧仮名遣い -> 現代仮名遣い sound changes the app's lookup-query normaliser
applies (#81): ハ行転呼 (ふ->う, ひ->い, へ->え, は->わ), アウ->オウ (やう->よう,
かう->こう) and エウ->ヨウ (けふ->きょう, てふ->ちょう). The rules rewrite the
queried string only; displayed text is never changed.

Source
------
The correspondences are those of two Cabinet notices (内閣告示), published by the
Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁):

  昭和21年内閣告示第33号「現代かなづかい」(1946-11-16)
    語中・語尾のハ行の仮名はワ行の仮名に書き改める; 母音の変化 (アウ->オウ,
    エウ->ヨウ)

  昭和61年内閣告示第1号「現代仮名遣い」(1986-07-01)
    本文第1 (直音・拗音・促音・長音の書き表し方) and 本文第2 (表記の慣習による
    特例: the particle は stays は, the verb いう（言） keeps いう, 「ぢ」「づ」,
    オ列の長音は「お」) — the source of the grammatical conditions the normaliser
    applies (e.g. the particle は never folds, 言ふ -> 言う keeps いう).

  https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gendaikana/index.html
  https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gendaikana/honbun_dai1.html
  https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gendaikana/honbun_dai2.html
  (retrieved 2026-09-14)

choices — which rows are withheld and which ending conditions apply — are
measured on the #75 bench slice and documented in the generator, the PROVENANCE
file and KanaSoundChangesTest.kt; they are not copied from the notices.
